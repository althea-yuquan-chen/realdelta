This text replaces a first draft of the same date that was reviewed before any run (review: restriction on the

## What round 1 suggested
From round-1 outputs (summary.txt) and an exploratory diagnostic on them that used no new seeds
(plan_from_validation_run/diag_failures_posthoc.txt):
- P-pooled (c = 2) failed 4 bias and 3 MAE decision cells, all Beta(20,1) with m = 10 cases.
- Its error, split by the number of failed trials in the user's file (F = N - X), over all decision cells: F = 0 (m = 10): mean error +0.150, p90 |error| 0.459; F 1-2: +0.048 (m = 10),
  +0.120 (m = 30); F 3-5: -0.042 (m = 10), +0.044 (m = 30), p90 0.19 and 0.18; F 6-10: +0.010 and -0.016,
  p90 0.14 and 0.12; F > 10: -0.010 and -0.003, p90 0.11 and 0.10. Plug-in shows the same pattern.
- The m = 30 cells passed on average, but the p90 of single-file error reached 0.232 there.
- P-Jeffreys and P-uniform underestimated power by 0.14 and 0.19 on average.
Reading: the error is not a function of F alone. Within one F band the sign differs between m = 10 and m = 30
(F 3-5: -0.042 vs +0.044), which fits an effect of F relative to its expected value (files that happen to show
few failures look more stable than their suite is, and the reverse). What the data do support is that files with
few observed failures give large single-file errors. Failures in a case that never passed say nothing about the
near-100% cases that drive break-k power, so the gate counts F* = failed trials in cases with at least one pass.
An exploratory check on round-1 data with this gate (plan_from_validation_r2_prep/diag_gate_fstar_posthoc.txt;
in-sample, not evidence for round 2): P-pooled met the bias and MAE criteria in every cell at F* >= 3, 6 and 10,
mean error between -0.036 and +0.038; cells with p90 > 0.20: 2, 0, 0. P-uniform failed many cells at every gate.

## Fixed now
Candidate: P-pooled exactly as in round 1 (per-case Beta(x_i + c*pbar, n_i - x_i + c*(1 - pbar)),
pbar = (X + 0.5)/(N + 1), c = 2). No other c is tried.
Controls:
- Oracle (true rates): positive control. |mean error| <= 0.03 in every cell (all suites, no gate), else void.
- P-uniform: negative control (round-1 mean error -0.19). If it meets every criterion at the chosen F_min, the
  criteria cannot reject a known-bad method and the validation is void.
- Plug-in: reported. It cannot be chosen.
Consistency check (owed by round 1, Revision 2, and not run then): for 200 random files per distribution
(seed 20261202), the internal _delta_interval on count arrays and the public delta_interval on outcome dicts

Seed: base 20261203 (round 1: 20261124). Same seed derivation as round 1. A smoke test of the pipeline

## Gate and decision rule
Gate: the tool refuses a file with F* < F_min, F* = failed trials in cases with at least one pass. F_min is chosen
from {3, 6, 10}. For each candidate, the truth phase (which knows F* of each file but runs no method) lists:
- gated suites: those with F* >= F_min;
- decision cells: (setting, change, n_plan) with at least 20 gated suites and mean true power over the gated
  suites in [0.2, 0.9];
- coverage: the candidate is eligible only if (a) for each m in {20, 50, 120}, the near-100% distributions
  (Beta(20,1), Mix, Beta(50,1), Mix95, Mix90Z) together have at least 2 decision cells, one of them with
  n_obs = 3; (b) Beta(50,1), Mix95 and Mix90Z each have at least 2 decision cells with m in {50, 120}; (c) at
  least 8 decision cells overall; (d) Beta(50,1), Mix95 and Mix90Z each have at least 1 decision cell with at
  least 50 gated suites, so the p90 criterion is tested on each. Decision cells are listed by distribution and m.
These lists are saved before the methods phase. If no candidate is eligible, the grid of k, delta and n_plan may
be adjusted once (the truth phase holds no method information), written as Revision A and fixed before the
methods phase; otherwise the validation is void.
Criteria for P-pooled, on gated suites, in every decision cell:
- mean error (estimate - truth) in [-0.10, +0.05]. Asymmetric because overestimating power leads a user to run
  too few trials and miss a real change; underestimating costs extra runs. Both bounds are fixed now; a failure
  at the lower bound counts as a failure;
- MAE <= 0.15;
- 90th percentile of |error| <= 0.20, in decision cells with at least 50 gated suites (new: a user has one file;
  threshold set after seeing round 1). With no Monte Carlo margin this criterion can fail on noise in one cell;
  that risk is accepted;
- false alarm, per (setting, n_plan) with at least 20 gated suites: mean error within +-0.03 (non-binding in round 1, where
  no method failed it; reported as such).
Point estimates, no Monte Carlo margin (with 100 suites the SE of a cell's mean error was 0.004-0.037 in round 1
with 50).
F_min: the smallest eligible candidate at which P-pooled meets every criterion. If none: nothing ships, shelved.
If Plug-in also meets every criterion at F_min, the write-up states that the criteria did not separate P-pooled
from Plug-in (round 1's Revision 2 reading: their power to discriminate is in question) and claims no advantage
for P-pooled; P-pooled still ships because it is the method that was validated as a candidate.

Reported, not decisive:
- refusal rate (share of files with F* < F_min) per setting: how often the tool would decline;
- per decision cell: gated suites, whether the p90 criterion applies, p90 of |error| for every method; per
  distribution x m: p90-constrained cells / decision cells. Where a distribution x m has no p90-constrained cell,
  the write-up says the p90 criterion was not tested there;
- the worst decision-cell p90 of P-pooled, given in the write-up as an upper bound next to the banded values
  (bands pool suite-cells of the same suite across changes and n_plan, so suite-cells are not independent);
- the paired per-suite |error| of P-pooled minus Plug-in;
- first n_plan in {n_obs, 5, 10, 20} reaching power 0.8, estimate vs truth, per method, gated suites.

## Validated range if shipped (nothing more is claimed)
At least F_min failed trials in cases with at least one pass (files with fewer are refused, and the message says
why); 20 to 120
cases (fewer is refused; more prints a note); 3 to 5 trials per case, equal across cases (fewer or unequal is
refused; more prints a note); planned trials up to 20; conf 0.95; break k in {1, 3} and shift -0.05 (other
changes print a note that they are outside the validated range). The write-up lists only the distribution and m
combinations that had decision cells at the chosen F_min, not all nine distributions.
Next to each power estimate the tool prints the validated single-file error for files like the user's: the p90
of P-pooled's |error| over gated suites of all decision cells whose F* falls in the same band as the user's file.
Bands: [F_min, 10), [10, 20), [20, inf) (empty ones dropped); a band with fewer than 100 suite-cells merges into
the band below it (the lowest band merges upward).
Not a coverage claim; does not touch the gated grid.
